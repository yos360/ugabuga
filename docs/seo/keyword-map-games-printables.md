## Holiday coloring (owned by the holiday pages)
`/holidays/hanukkah/coloring` owns "דף צביעה חנוכה" (2,900) / "דפי צביעה לחנוכה" (2,400); `/holidays/purim/coloring` owns "דפי צביעה לפורים" (2,400); `/holidays/tu-bishvat/coloring` owns "דפי צביעה לט״ו בשבט". The subject pages sufganiyah, dreidel, hanukkiah, tu-bishvat and purim-masks were retitled to an item angle (סופגנייה לצביעה, סביבון לצביעה…) and now link back to their holiday coloring page.

## Why /printables/coloring had zero impressions
The page is indexable and prerendered (title, canonical, H1, 48 subject links all in the HTML). The coloring section was rebuilt on 2026-10-08 (subject pages added that day), so Google had ~3 days; the subject pages also show no impressions yet. "דפי צביעה" is a 40,500 head term where a young site typically ranks beyond the first pages, which records no impressions. Fixes applied: stronger title/description with the variants people search, intro mentioning דפי צביעה לילדים, a "which page to choose" body section linking 17 subject/holiday pages with keyword anchors, two extra FAQ entries, 30 card titles demoted from h2 to h3, sitemap priority 0.7 → 0.9 with lastmod, new home/printables/search links with the anchor "דפי צביעה".
