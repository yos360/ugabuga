## Thin or near-duplicate pages (left in place, owner to decide)

- /languages/{fr,es,ru,ar,am}/quiz: about 145 words each, and 64% of their text is shared. Recommendation: noindex them, or fold the quiz into each language's home page.
- /letters/* (23 pages): about 230 words, and only about 50% of each page is unique text. Recommendation: add an original word list and a short tip for each letter, as /abc/* already has (91% unique).
- /math topic pages are 77% unique (explanation, tips and a worked example for each topic). The /math/tests/* pages share about 50% of their text, which is acceptable because each one generates its own quiz.
- /languages/<lang>/<topic> (88% unique), /english/* (86%), /animals/* (99%) and /abc/* (91%) are fine.
