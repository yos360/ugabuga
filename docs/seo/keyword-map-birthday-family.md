## Cannibalization resolved

1. /ideas/without-entertainer and /guides/birthday-without-entertainer had the same H1. The guide was renamed to "איך מפעילים יום הולדת לבד".
2. /ideas/at-home and /guides/birthday-at-home-complete both targeted "יום הולדת בבית". The guide now covers organizing the home.
3. /toddlers/birthday-age-1, /ideas/first-birthday and /guides/first-birthday-guide all targeted "יום הולדת שנה". The toddlers page is the owner.
4. /holidays/rosh-hashana/greetings and /holidays/blessings/rosh-hashana both targeted "ברכות לראש השנה". The greetings page is the owner and the blessings page now targets the liturgy.
5. /holidays/X/what-to-do and the /ideas/X-activity pages, for Rosh Hashana, Shavuot, Sukkot, Pesach, Lag BaOmer, Hanukkah and Purim. The holidays pages are the owners. Each ideas page got a distinct group or party angle and an "עוד בנושא" link to its owner.
6. /birthday-greetings and /greeting both targeted "ברכה ליום הולדת". The hub is the owner and the generator keeps the "מחולל" angle.
