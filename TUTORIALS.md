# How-to tutorials — עוגה בוגה ("איך משתמשים בעוגה בוגה")

Landscape screen-recorded walkthroughs of real site tools, uploaded to **YouTube only**, into the playlist
"איך משתמשים בעוגה בוגה". They are separate from the daily reel (which stays as it is).
One tutorial per day, prepared in the cloud at 10:05 and uploaded from the owner's Chrome at 12:00.

## Rules
- Record the REAL site (built from `main`), not drawings. Every label, button and number on screen is real.
- 1920×1080 (16:9), 30 fps, 60–120 s, H.264 + AAC, < 60 MB. Quiet background music (synthesized, LUFS −18) — no voice.
- Structure: 3 s title card (site look: paper `#fffefa`, ink `#14162d`, Heebo; title "איך <עושים X> בעוגה בוגה") →
  4–7 numbered steps recorded in the browser, each with a Hebrew caption bar at the bottom (step number + short sentence) →
  5 s end card (logo, "עוגה בוגה", the full URL in a blue pill, "בחינם, בלי הרשמה").
- Show the mouse/taps clearly (highlight ring on clicks). Slow, readable pace: ~2 s pause after each result.
- Never show personal data. Use example names like "נועה", "כיתה ב׳ 2".
- YouTube title ≤ 100 chars: "איך <…> — מדריך קצר | עוגה בוגה" (no #shorts). Description: 2–3 lines, the steps as a list with times
  (chapters 0:00 …), the link, "בחינם, בלי הרשמה", 3–5 hashtags starting with #עוגה_בוגה.
